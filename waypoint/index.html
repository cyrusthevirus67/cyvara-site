How it works

Five steps from unsure to underway.

Waypoint doesn't hand you a list of job titles. It walks with you from self-understanding all the way to a decision and a plan.

  • Understand yourself
  • Explore possibilities
  • Test them in the real world
  • Make decisions
  • Move forward

01

Understand yourself

Waypoint starts with you — your strengths, interests, values, and constraints — through real conversation, not a 200-question quiz.

You get a clear picture of what drives you

02

Explore possibilities

See paths you'd never think to search for, and understand what each one is actually like day to day.

You get a shortlist worth your time

03

Test them in the real world

Small, low-risk experiments — conversations to have, projects to try, skills to sample — so you learn by doing before you commit.

You get evidence, not guesses

04

Make decisions

Compare your options side by side against what matters to you, with every tradeoff out in the open.

You get a decision you can stand behind

05

Move forward

Leave with a next step and a plan, not just an insight. Waypoint keeps track of where you are and what comes after.

You get momentum

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Waypoint?
Waypoint is an AI life and career navigator. It helps you understand yourself, explore careers, test them in the real world, and decide what to do next.
Is Waypoint available yet?
Not yet. We're building it now. Join the early access list and we'll email you when early access opens.
Do I need to know what I want to do?
No. Waypoint is built for people who don't have the answer yet. That's the whole point.
How much will it cost?
We haven't announced pricing yet. Joining the early access list is free.
What happens to the information I share?
We use what you share to run your Waypoint experience and to contact you about Waypoint. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
Who makes Waypoint?
Waypoint is the first product from Cyvara, an AI software company building products that help people make better decisions and navigate complexity.
